Curriculum & Courses

Explore our comprehensive classical Bharatanatyam syllabus, spanning from foundational steps to professional mastery.

Back to Dashboard
Foundation Level #01

Fresh Admission (Beginner)

Introduction to basic body postures (Araimandi, Samapada), fundamental hand gestures (Asamyutta Hastas), eye movements, and foundational Adavus (Tatta Adavu).

Elementary Level #02

Prarambhik

Mastery of primary Adavus including Natta Adavu, Kuditta Mettu, and Paraval Adavu. Introduction to basic Tala rhythm concepts and introductory slokas from Abhinaya Darpana.

Intermediate I #03

Praveshika Pratham

Advanced Adavu patterns, speed variations (Kala), introductory Korvais, Samyutta Hastas (combined hand gestures), and head/neck movements (Shiro & Greeva Bheda).

Intermediate II #04

Praveshika Purna

Introduction to simple repertoire items like Alarippu (Tisra, Chatusra, or Misra) and Kautuvam. Basic understanding of Natyashastra theory and rhythm cycles.

Junior Diplomatic I #05

Madhyama Pratham

Performance of Jatiswaram and Shabdam. Nuances of expressive dance (Abhinaya), character portrayal, and study of musical ragas and talas.

Junior Diplomatic II #06

Madhyama Purna

Detailed training in central repertoire pieces such as Varnam. Balancing intricate Nritta (pure dance footwork) with deeper emotive Sanchari Bhavas.

Senior Graduate I #07

Visharad Pratham

In-depth Varnam choreography, Padams, and Ashtapadis. Advanced study of Navarasas (nine classical emotions), Natya theory, and musical accompaniment.

Senior Graduate II #08

Visharad Dwitiya

Advanced repertoire including complex Padams, Keerthanams, and energetic Tillanas. Theory on stage presentation, Aharya (costumes/makeup), and Tala calculations.

Pre-Graduation Level #09

Visharad Tritiya

Mastery of full Margam performance. Detailed analytical study of classical treatises, choreographic structuring, and Nattuvangam rhythm recital.

Mastery & Artistry #10

Alankar Pratham

Post-graduate professional training focusing on thematic productions, solo stage presence, lecture demonstrations, and pedagogical mastery for future gurus.

Art. Discipline. Devotion.
